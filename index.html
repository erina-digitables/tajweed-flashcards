How to use it

Each round takes a few minutes. Do one at the start of every lesson, or whenever you have time to practise.

  1. 1

    Read the question

    Say your answer out loud, or in your head, before you look.

  2. 2

    Flip the card

    Tap the card, or press Space, to see the answer.

  3. 3

    Be honest

    Choose Got it if you knew it, or Still learning if you didn't.

  4. 4

    Redo your mistakes

    At the end, pick Redo mistakes only and keep going until you get every card right.
